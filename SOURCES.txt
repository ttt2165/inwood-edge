1. TEMPLATE
The site is built on the course template, astro-maplibre-template
(github.com/design-practices/astro-maplibre-template). I kept its page
layout, header, map components and GitHub Pages deployment. I removed
the Scrollytelling page from the menu and replaced the sample maps.

2. TUTORIALS
Tutorials 8.1–8.3 (Interactive Mapping Parts 1–3) showed me how to set up
the site, style the basemap in Maputnik (public/style.json), write map
YAML files, classify layers and add popups.

3. MY DATA AND ANALYSIS
The map layers come from my Assignment 2 and 3 QGIS work: the 1924
shoreline and water, 1924 and 1951 land use (my reading of the city
aerials), homes and facilities in the 2080s floodplain, place names and
Flood Vulnerability Index tracts. The argument and the layer choices
come from my atlas proposal.

4. AI ASSISTANCE (Perplexity)
I used Perplexity, an AI assistant, extensively. It:
- converted my QGIS layers to GeoJSON and built new layers from NYC open
  data (made land, (E)-designated lots, new-building filings since 2018,
  the Special Inwood District);
- wrote the map YAML files (src/maps/full-page.yaml and the four story
  maps), the inline maps page text and the map note;
- pushed the changes to my GitHub repository from my Codespace.
I chose the layers and the story, reviewed the maps and wording, and
checked the counts against my proposal. Where its numbers differ from my
slides (new buildings: 20 vs. 16; (E) lots in FEMA: 12 vs. 29), I have
noted it.

5. DATA SOURCES
NYC Department of City Planning (MapPLUTO, Facilities Database,
(E) Designations, Special Purpose Districts, Borough Boundaries); FEMA
flood zones; NYC Flood Hazard Mapper (2080s floodplain); NYC Flood
Vulnerability Index; NYC Department of Buildings (DOB NOW and BIS
filings); NYC historical aerials (1924, 1951); basemap © OpenStreetMap
contributors, OpenFreeMap.
